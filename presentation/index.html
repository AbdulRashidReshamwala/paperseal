THE PROBLEM

An exam paper is only as safe as every person who touches it.

Today a paper passes through many hands before a single student sees it. Nobody can prove where it leaked, or that it didn’t.

  1. 01

    Setter

    Writes the questions

  2. 02

    Moderator

    Reviews the full paper

  3. 03

    Printer

    Holds every copy

  4. 04

    Courier

    Moves sealed boxes

  5. 05

    Storage

    Safes, strongrooms

  6. 06

    Centre

    Opens the packet

One person in this chain is enough. The system asks everyone to be trusted.
